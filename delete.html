Delete your data

LAST UPDATED 5 AUGUST 2026

Micron has no accounts. Everything the server holds about you is tied to a player code — a random string your phone made the first time you opened the app. Nobody, including us, can connect that code to a person.

What deleting removes

  • Every score you have submitted, on every day, in both pools.
  • The handle you chose, wherever it appears on a board.
  • Your membership of any friend group. A group left with no members is deleted too.

It happens immediately and cannot be undone. Your scores do not come back if you change your mind.

Delete it yourself

Open Micron, go to About, and copy your player code. Paste it here.

Or ask us

If you have uninstalled the app and no longer have your code, email mustafasalimerek@gmail.com from any address and say which handle and roughly which days were yours. We will find the rows and delete them. Expect a reply within seven days.

Uninstalling on its own does not delete anything from the server — it only abandons the code, and the old scores stay on the board under the handle.

What is not stored at all

No email, no name, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no payment details. There is nothing else to delete. See the privacy policy for what the ad SDK collects on its own account.

Silme neyi kaldırır

  • Gönderdiğin bütün skorlar, her gün, her iki havuzda.
  • Seçtiğin isim, tablolarda göründüğü her yerde.
  • Arkadaş gruplarındaki üyeliğin. Üyesi kalmayan grup da silinir.

İşlem anında gerçekleşir ve geri alınamaz. Fikrini değiştirirsen skorların geri gelmez.

Kendin sil

Micron'u aç, Hakkında bölümüne git, oyuncu kodunu kopyala. Buraya yapıştır.

Ya da bize yaz

Uygulamayı sildiysen ve kodun elinde yoksa, herhangi bir adresten mustafasalimerek@gmail.com adresine yaz; hangi ismi kullandığını ve yaklaşık hangi günlerde oynadığını söyle. Satırları bulup sileriz. Yanıt en geç yedi gün içinde.
