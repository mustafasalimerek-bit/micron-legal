The game

Micron is free to play. Every player — paying or not — gets the same seven daily tasks, the same board and the same ranking. Nothing sold here changes a score or a position on a leaderboard.

Micron Pro

Micron Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. It unlocks the archive of every past daily round, removes the forced ads shown between tasks, and makes hints free. Archive rounds are scored for you alone and are never posted to any leaderboard. It is sold in two lengths:

  • Monthly — billed every month.
  • Yearly — billed every year.

The price and currency are the ones shown inside the app before you confirm; they are set by the App Store or Google Play for your region and can differ from any figure quoted elsewhere.

Renewal. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before that period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period.

Cancelling. Manage or cancel the subscription in your own store account — Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on iOS, or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions on Android. Deleting the app does not cancel it. We cannot cancel it for you: the subscription is between you and the store.

Refunds are handled by the store that took the payment, under that store's policy, not by us.
